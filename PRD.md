# Product Overview:
A convenient food-ordering platform engineered for Babcock students to browse menus and order meals from the food vendors of their choice. This platform allows students to
schedule their orders and choose a pickup time that best fits their academic schedules and personal convenience. This will simplify the food-acquiring process, reduce waiting times, and allow them to select pickup times that better suit them.



**Problem Statement**: When buying food, students need to commute from their halls, classes, or wherever they are to supermarkets, food joints, or small food outlets and kiosks. When there, they often suffer harsh weather, unorganized crowds, or, better yet, long lines; this is ineffective, stressful, and time-consuming. Additionally, students who go to the University Cafeteria suffer similar fates in terms of crowding.



**Why This Problem Matters**
Food is a fundamental need for everyone, and for students, this truth proves even more crucial. Currently, many students spend too much time searching for meals alone, which takes a lot of time away from pursuing their academics, and challenges like harsh weather and frustrating crowds don't exactly improve the whole situation.

Number of Responses: 16

**Results**

*Question 1*: Do you often experience difficulty while buying or selling on campus?
Result: 75% of the respondents picked "Yes".


*Question 2*: How often do you experience these problems?
Result: 53.3% of the respondents said that they suffered from these issues several times in one week.


*Question 3*: How long have you experienced these difficulties?
Result: 50% of the respondents said that they had experienced these issues for less than a month, while 21.4% of the respondents said that they had experienced these issues for one to six months.


**Insights**
1. Overpopulation is a serious problem.
2. These problems have existed for a considerable period of time.
3. These problems occur to many students several times a week.

# Proposed Solution
Solution Summary\
Our solution is to allow students to order the food of their choice comfortably in any place of their choice and go and pick it up at an organized time slot to directly tackle the problem of overcrowding and poor vendor-to-customer interactions.

### A. Pre-order and pickup:
A student picks a vendor, selects items, and pays in the app. The vendor prepares the order, and the student gets a notification when it's ready. They walk over, show their order number, and leave.
Overcrowding: students arrive only when food is ready, so no line forms.
Overwhelmed staff: orders come in as a neat queue instead of a crowd.
Commuting: students can order from class or their hall and only walk over at the end.
### B. Vendor discovery page:
A single in-app directory of every campus seller: cafeteria, supermarkets, food joints, and kiosks. Each vendor shows:
- Menu and prices
- Opening hours and location
- Live status (open, sold out, ready in 10 mins)
Students stop making wasted trips, and small kiosks get the same visibility as the cafeteria.
